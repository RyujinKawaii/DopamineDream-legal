DopamineDream Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-15

1. Overview

DopamineDream is a personal desktop tool that uploads the operator's own rendered videos to their own TikTok account as drafts. It has no other users and does not collect personal data from the public.

2. Data we access from TikTok

  • user.info.basic: the account's open ID, display name and avatar, used only to confirm which account is connected.
  • video.upload: permission to send video files to the account's TikTok inbox as drafts.

3. How data is stored

OAuth access and refresh tokens are stored only on the operator's own computer (Windows Credential Manager). They are not sent to any server other than TikTok's API, and are never shared with third parties.

4. Data we do not collect

The tool does not collect, sell or share viewer data, analytics, contacts, location or any other personal information.

5. Deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time from TikTok's settings (Security and permissions → Apps and services). Revoking access and deleting the stored tokens removes all data held by the tool.
